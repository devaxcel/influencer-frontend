Influnexa: find, verify and hire creators, journalists and public figures

Find journalists, creators, speakers, experts and public figures. See their real reach per platform, then connect or hire. Verified profiles, open campaigns and signed agreements in one place, in English, Urdu and Arabic.

What you can do

  • Discover talent by name, profession, industry, topic, country, city, language and followers.
  • Shortlist and compare up to four people side by side.
  • Post a campaign and let verified talent apply with a pitch.
  • Sign a digital agreement with milestones; deliveries and approvals are tracked.

Pricing

Influnexa is free right now: search, accounts, claiming a profile, shortlists, hire requests, campaigns and agreements. Talent set their own prices per service (fixed, a range, or price on request), shown on each profile.

How to book

Open a talent profile and press Contact / Hire, or send a question without an account. Inquiries are open 24/7 and the team replies by email within 1–2 working days.

Questions, answered

How is a profile verified?

The person claims the profile, we send a 6-digit code to one of their official accounts, and an admin approves it once they enter the code.

Is it free to join?

Creating an account and a profile is free.

How does hiring work?

A verified business sends a hire request. When the talent accepts, both sides see each other's contact details and can draft an agreement.

Can a manager work for a creator?

Yes. A verified manager can request to represent a creator, and the creator chooses what the manager may do.

My profile is already listed. What now?

Find it, press Claim and follow the steps. Once approved it shows the verified badge and you control the details.
