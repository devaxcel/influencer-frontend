## Our mission

Finding the right voice for a campaign, event or story is slow and full of guesswork. Numbers are scattered across apps, it is hard to know who to contact, and deals are made over chats with nothing in writing.

We put the whole journey in one place: discover people with transparent reach, hire them directly or through an open campaign, agree on terms in a signed digital agreement, track the work milestone by milestone, and review each other when it is done.

## How it works

1. **Discover:** search talent by profession, industry, topic, country, language and reach, or browse open campaigns.
2. **Connect and hire:** a verified business sends a hire request for a service, or talents apply to its campaign. Contacts open up on both sides once the talent says yes.
3. **Agree and sign:** both sides shape the terms, milestones and amounts in one agreement, then sign it with a one-time code sent to their email.
4. **Deliver and review:** the talent delivers each milestone, the business approves or asks for changes, and when it is complete both leave a star review.

## Built for three kinds of accounts

- **Talent:** claim or create your profile and show a verified badge; list your services, prices and availability; get hire requests and apply to open campaigns; sign agreements and build your star rating.
- **Managers and agents:** get verified as a manager or agency; link with your talents, with their approval; manage their profiles and services; answer all their hire requests from one inbox.
- **Businesses and organizations:** companies, brands, agencies, NGOs, universities, event organizers and government bodies. Get verified, then hire talent directly or post a campaign; shortlist and compare people with real numbers; work under signed agreements and leave reviews.

## What we stand for

- **Verified people:** talents, managers and businesses are verified by our team before they can hire, represent or edit a profile.

## Questions people ask

### Where does the profile information come from?
From public sources such as official websites and social accounts. After a person claims their profile, they keep it up to date themselves.

### How do I claim my profile?
Create a talent account, open your profile and press "Is this you? Claim it". We send a code to one of your official social accounts; enter it on your dashboard. If you can't find your profile, create one yourself with at least one social account.

### What does the verified badge mean?
The profile was claimed by the real person and confirmed with a code or by our team. Businesses and managers are verified separately before they can hire or represent anyone.

### How do campaigns work?
A verified business posts a campaign. Talents with a profile apply with a short pitch. The business selects or rejects applicants; a selected talent gets an accepted hire request and the agreement starts from there.

### How are agreements signed and paid?
Both sides agree on the terms and milestones, then each signs with a code sent to their email. Payment is arranged between the two sides for now; the platform keeps the signed record, deliveries and approvals.

### Who can leave a review?
Only the two sides of a completed agreement. That is why every star on a talent's profile comes from real work.

### Something on a profile is wrong. What can I do?
Open the profile and use "Report an error or request removal". Our team reviews every report.
