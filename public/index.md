# Influnexa: discover the people who influence your world

Find journalists, creators, speakers, experts and public figures. See their real reach, then connect or hire. Built for brands, talent and managers: verified profiles, open campaigns and signed agreements in one place. Available in English, Urdu and Arabic.

## What you can do

- **Discover talent:** search by name, profession, industry, topic, country, city, language and followers. Every verified profile shows its sources openly. https://influnexa.com/search ([markdown](https://influnexa.com/search.md))
- **Shortlist, compare and decide:** keep a named list for every campaign, add private notes, and compare up to four people side by side.
- **Post a campaign and let talent come to you:** describe the job once; verified talent applies with a pitch. https://influnexa.com/campaigns ([markdown](https://influnexa.com/campaigns.md))
- **Sign it properly, pay by milestone:** agree on terms and milestones, both sides sign with a code, deliveries and approvals are tracked.

## How it works

1. **Discover:** search by name, profession, industry, topic or city.
2. **Check real reach:** followers and engagement per platform, with the source shown.
3. **Connect or hire:** send a question (no account needed) or a hire request from a verified business account, then sign an agreement.

## Questions, answered

### How is a profile verified?
The person claims the profile, we send a 6-digit code to one of their official accounts, and an admin approves it once they enter the code.

### Is it free to join?
Creating an account and a profile is free.

### How does hiring work?
A verified business sends a hire request. When the talent accepts, both sides see each other's contact details and can draft an agreement.

### Can a manager work for a creator?
Yes. A verified manager can request to represent a creator, and the creator chooses what the manager may do.

### My profile is already listed. What now?
Find it, press Claim and follow the steps. Once approved it shows the verified badge and you control the details.
